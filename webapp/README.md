# 🎙️ አጋዥ (Agazh) — Amharic Voice AI Mini App

Telegram Mini App for the Telegram Translator & Voice Bot.

---

## 🚀 How to Deploy to Vercel (Free in 1 minute)

### Option A: Via GitHub (Recommended)
1. Go to [vercel.com](https://vercel.com) and log in with GitHub.
2. Click **"Add New..."** → **"Project"**.
3. Select your repository: `telegram-translator-bot`.
4. In **Root Directory**, click "Edit" and choose `webapp`.
5. Click **"Deploy"**!
6. Copy your deployed domain (e.g. `https://your-app.vercel.app`).

---

## 📱 How to Connect to Your Bot in BotFather

1. Open Telegram and message `@BotFather`.
2. Tap your bot: **`Redster (@R3dSter_Bot)`**.
3. Tap **`Bot Settings`** → **`Menu Button`** (or **`Mini Apps`** → **`Menu Button`**).
4. Tap **`Configure Menu Button`**.
5. Paste your Vercel URL: `https://your-app.vercel.app`.
6. Set the button text to: `🎙️ Voice AI` (or `አጋዥ Voice`).
7. **Done!** Open your bot in Telegram — the **🎙️ Voice AI** button is now live right next to the chat bar!
